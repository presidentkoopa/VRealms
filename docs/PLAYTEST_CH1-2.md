# Try this in the headset: chapters 1-2 (milestone 3)

For the owner. About 20 minutes. Start a new game with "PLAY VREALMS VR.bat". Everything here is built, and most of it
was seen working on your PC in test runs without the headset (the knock and the Book were not confirmed there); none
of it has been judged in the headset. For each line, the part after the arrow
is what should happen. Anything that doesn't happen, feels wrong, is too slow, or is awkward to do is worth
reporting, even if it's just a few words ("door feels floaty", "the match never lights").

Your hands: **trigger = look at / examine, grip = use / take.** Point at something far away, or touch it with your hand.

## In the front of the house (chapter 1)

1. **Walk, turn, crouch.** Stick to walk, snap turn 45 degrees; crouch for real → Adam crouches.
2. **Touch a wall hard.** → your hand stops at the wall instead of going through it, with a buzz.
3. **The Colt and its magazines.** Take them by reaching and squeezing → the item flies into your hand as a 3D model.
   Put the Colt on a hip, shoulder or chest → it holsters there; reach back and grip → it comes out.
4. **Shoot the Colt** → the shot leaves where the gun points, not where you look.
5. **Drop something** (open your hand away from your shoulder) → it falls and lies on the floor. **Pick it up again**
   (squeeze near it). **Throw it** → it flies and lands. **Over your shoulder** and let go → it goes into your pack.
6. **The Book** → it opens in both hands; the silver key drops into your palm.
7. **The Matchbox in one hand, strike a match with the other** along the box's side → a lit match for about 20 s.
   Light the tall candles **wick by wick** → each wick catches, the light grows.
8. **The Lamp** → it hangs on your hip and lights around you; grab it to hold it up.
9. **Bring an item close to your eyes** → its description plays. **Knock twice** on something with an open hand →
   Adam comments on it.
10. **The brass key from the niche, then the Study door**: hold the key at the lock and turn it → the door opens.
    **Grab a door by its latch side and swing it** → it follows your hand; let go mid-swing → it keeps swinging.
    Push an open door shut by hand → it closes.

## The Study and below (chapter 2)

11. **The first creatures.** Punch one → it takes damage. **The Sword**: swing it (the trigger does nothing with it)
    → hits land only when you really swing. **The Shield** → it sits on your other forearm.
12. **The Shotgun**: put your other hand on the fore-end → you hold it with two hands, and it aims along the barrel.
13. **The bookcase**: pull its edge → it opens.
14. **Levers** (store rooms, mausoleum) → grab and pull them through their swing.
15. **Key i22 in the lock lever** (mausoleum) → turn it like a key.
16. **The hand prints** (the gauntlet room) → press your open palm into each one.
17. **The Orb into its door, the serpent statuettes onto their plates** → put them there and open your hand.
18. **A vial** → tip it at your mouth to drink.
19. **The throne** → turn around and actually sit down.
20. **The Shrive** at Aelf's door → set it in and turn it half a turn.

A line per thing that's off, in your own words. Also: what felt best, and what you expected to be able to do but
couldn't. That last list is the most useful one for what comes next.

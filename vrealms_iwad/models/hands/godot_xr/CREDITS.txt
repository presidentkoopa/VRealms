THE VR HANDS: the Godot XR Tools hand models

Model: the "hand_r" and "hand_l" hands of Godot XR Tools (https://github.com/GodotVR/godot-xr-tools,
addons/godot-xr-tools/hands/model/hand_r.gltf and hand_l.gltf, commit 6c6189bdfcdf855052ef1aeb9f3e0ec4c7a7eb60).
Skin: addons/godot-xr-tools/hands/textures/caucasian_realistic_baseColor.png, unchanged.

Author: DigitalN8m4r3 (Miodrag Sejic). Made with MakeHuman Community Edition, slightly modified in Blender and
textured in Substance Painter (the hands' About.md).

What VRealms did to them (godot_xr_hands.fbx, written by tools/hand_retarget/retarget_godot.py): the two glTF meshes
and their skins put into one FBX with the joint names and the take of the owner's hand_final.fbx, every frame of that
take carried onto these hands as joint angles in each rig's own finger frames (the thumb also kept where it touches
the fingers), scaled to the owner's 0.19 m from the wrist to the middle fingertip.

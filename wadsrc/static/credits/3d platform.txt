3D Platform by FishyClock, GPL-3.0, https://github.com/FishyClock/3Dplatform

A ZScript library: solid moving and rotating platform actors that carry what
stands on them, pushable crates, platform groups and portal awareness.
Copyright (C) 2022-2026 Fishytza A.K.A. FishyClockwork.

Its two core script files are shipped unmodified in zscript/fishy/, with the
library's own licence text beside them (zscript/fishy/LICENSE.txt). The engine
uses it for Realms of the Haunting's moving sectors (cvar roth_platforms):
the platform class LoaderPlatform in zscript/actors/loaderplatform.zs derives
from the library's FishyPlatform.
